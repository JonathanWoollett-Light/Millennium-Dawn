Portugal starts in 2000 with a socialist government, membership of the European Union, the
Eurozone and NATO, five divisions of which two garrison islands, and a debt eight times the
size of its treasury. It also starts with more influence in six former colonies than any
other power holds there, and no instrument between a diplomatic note and a war.

The tree is about building that instrument.

## Political Situation

The Socialist Party governs with 45.2% support. The Social Democrats are the alternative
government at 33.1%, the CDU holds 9.2% on the left and the CDS-PP 8.5% on the right. The
Braganças and the national right are rounding errors that some branches of the tree do not
leave as rounding errors.

## The Tree

Eleven branches in three rows: the state and the three services above, the programme of
government in the middle, the empire below.

- **A Terceira República** — state reform, corruption, the diaspora, and four party roads
  gated on who won the last election.
- **A Questão Ideológica** — an ideological development meter that rises on every change of
  government and every policy enacted, and gates the radical content behind it; focuses that
  move popularity, put people in the street and, at the far end, replace the government
  outside the electoral calendar; and alignment focuses that open Beijing, Washington, the
  sovereigntist bloc, BRICS or the Lusophone world depending on who is in office.
- **O Programa de Governo** — the policy tree: financial, industrial and social policy, each
  a set of two-sided forks. Taking a side commits the country to it, and reversing it needs
  the other bloc to win a majority first. The reversal undoes the focuses as well as the
  effects, so an optimal set of policies means flipping governments deliberately.
- **O Programa de Convergência** — the euro, the debt and reindustrialisation.
- **As Forças Armadas**, **O Mar Português** and **A Força Aérea** — professionalisation,
  procurement, and a doctrine school in each service. Nothing is mutually exclusive; the
  time and the money make the choice.
- **A Comunidade Lusófona** — the Legion, the war school, the European question and the
  Fifth Empire.
- **A Intervenção** — the shadow war in three grades.
- **As Campanhas** — one chain per target, built on what actually happened there.
- **As Potências** — the near-peers and the great powers, where the instruments do not work
  and the only leverage is what Portugal owns that they want.

## The Shadow War

The loop is: cultivate a network in a former colony, buy its officer corps, sponsor a rising,
send volunteers to the side you created, and puppet whoever wins.

Backing the weaker side, or the side the world has outlawed, pays more influence and more
reward, and raises the exposure meter faster. Exposure is what Brussels sees. Past a
threshold it counts against Portugal in the European Union's own breach machinery, and at
tier three an ultimatum forces the choice between standing down and leaving.

Overseas intervention is priced through logistics rather than a fee: fuel consumption and
unit attrition scale with the number of theatres Portuguese troops are standing in, which
falls hardest on formations Portugal cannot replace.

**Intervention** builds the instruments: a foreign intelligence service, an operations
section, civilian and military networks, and the Special Actions Detachment.

**Neo-colonialism** buys mining concessions, a defence agreement and an economic
protectorate, and opens two ways of starting a war somebody else fights. The directed rising
is an operation, not a decision: it needs forty network strength in the target, both the
civilian and the army infiltration tokens, more than ten per cent influence and a network
above half, and it starts the civil war with Portuguese politics in the rebel government.

**The campaigns** are the list of places to use the instruments, eight chains of three
focuses each. Angola is the war that is already running on day one and Portugal observes it
from Estoril with a foot in both camps. Guinea-Bissau is the coup state, where every officer
above major did his staff course in Lisbon and two tonnes of cocaine a week come ashore in
the Bijagós. Mozambique is the dam Portugal still owned in 2000, the training mission and the
Rovuma gas. East Timor only exists from 2002. Equatorial Guinea joins the club in 2014 and
brings the Black Beach file with it. Cabinda is not a country yet. Cape Verde is the one
where nobody gets shot, and São Tomé is one island at the point every West African tanker
has to pass.

**The powers** are the two tiers above that. Near-peers cannot be subverted, so the hostile
move against Spain, France, the United Kingdom or the Union is a veto, a leak, a competing
offer in a third country or a refusal to sign — the Iberian summits against de-Spanishing, the
alliance of 1386, the Brussels corridors, the CPLP against the Francophonie. Great powers
cannot be subverted or outvoted either, so the leverage is Lajes, the Sines terminal, the grid
and the Portuguese half of China's Lusophone diplomacy. Lajes can be renegotiated, taken to the
Azores summit of March 2003, or simply not renewed, which is the only hostile act a country
this size has against one that size and can be done once.

**The open hand** drops the pretence. Marines evacuate Portuguese nationals from a coast
where the shooting has started and the garrison stays on the province they evacuated into.
When the fighting stops, whether anyone asks for it back depends on whether the side Portugal
backed won. At the far end, and only with fictional content enabled, there is a file on
Madrid, and winning it opens Galicia.

## AI Behaviour

The `POR_ai_behavior` game rule offers Historical, Lusophone, Imperial, Random Path and No
Path. Historical Portugal works the economy and the policy tree and leaves the networks
alone. Lusophone runs the cultivation and the puppeting. Imperial takes it to the Fifth
Empire.
